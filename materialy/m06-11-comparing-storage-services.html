Comparing Storage Services

In this lesson, you will learn how to do the following:

  • Explain how a company might use AWS storage services for different real-world scenarios.

In this Cloud in Real Life video, you will learn about three real-world scenarios involving Amazon S3, Amazon EBS, and Amazon EFS. Our AWS instructors will explain how these services solve specific business challenges for different industries. By the end, you will understand the key differences between the different AWS storage services and their ideal use cases.
