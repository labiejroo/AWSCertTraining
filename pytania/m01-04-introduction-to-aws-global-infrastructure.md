## Question 1

You just joined a tech start-up, and the business is growing rapidly. Your new company decides that they need to design a resilient and scalable infrastructure on AWS to handle increased traffic and help ensure high availability.Which statement BEST describes the AWS Global Infrastructure benefit of high availability?

_Choose 1_

- [ ] A. AWS has many customer support options to make sure the answers to your questions are highly available on its website.
- [x] B. AWS provides multiple data centers across different geographic regions so your website can remain operational even if one location faces issues.
- [ ] C. AWS offers a single, highly secure data center that can handle all your traffic, which makes sure that your website is available.
- [ ] D. AWS stores all of your website’s data in a single AWS storage bucket to centralize data management.

<!-- klucz: znaczniki przy odpowiedziach -->

> Correct! Rationale: AWS does provide multiple data centers across different geographic regions. This setup enhances high availability by making sure that your online store can handle increased traffic even during unforeseen events.
