## Question 1

Generative AI is a type of deep learning powered by extremely large ML models that are pre-trained on vast collections of data. What are these models called?

_Choose 1_

- [ ] A. Generative models
- [ ] B. Massive models
- [x] C. Foundation models
- [ ] D. Feature models

<!-- klucz: znaczniki przy odpowiedziach -->

> Correct! Rationale: Generative AI is powered by extremely large ML models known as foundation models (FMs). FMs are pre-trained on vast collections of data. FMs can be adapted to perform multiple tasks.
