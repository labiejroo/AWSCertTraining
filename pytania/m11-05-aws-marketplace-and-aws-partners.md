## Question 1

You are a new cloud solutions architect at a growing tech company. Your manager has asked you to explore the AWS Marketplace to find solutions that can help improve your company's operational efficiency and reduce costs. Based on what you've learned, which options are available on the AWS Marketplace?

_Choose 2_

- [x] A. Software as a service (SaaS) applications
- [ ] B. Cloud storage
- [ ] C. Virtual machines (VMs)
- [x] D. Datasets and analytics tools
- [ ] E. Hardware products for on-premises deployment

<!-- klucz: znaczniki przy odpowiedziach -->

> Correct! Rationale: The AWS Marketplace offers a wide range of SaaS applications that the company can subscribe to and use directly within their AWS environment. It also provides various datasets and analytics tools that can help gain insights and make data-driven decisions.
