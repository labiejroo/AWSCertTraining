Question -

A retail business plans to launch a new line of clothing, but they are struggling with accurately predicting how much server capacity they will need to support the launch.Which benefit of the AWS Cloud is most relevant to this situation?

Choose 1.
A. Stop spending money to run and maintain data centers.
B. Stop guessing capacity.
C. Trade upfront expense for variable expense.
D. Go global in minutes.
Correct!
Rationale: With the AWS Cloud, the company can conveniently scale resources up or down based on actual demand, eliminating the need to guess future capacity requirements.
